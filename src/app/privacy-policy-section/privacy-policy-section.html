Privacy Policy

1. Data Protection at a Glance

This application ("Join") uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide its core functionality: user accounts, contacts, and task management. This policy explains exactly what data is collected, why, and how it is processed.

2. Who is Responsible

Data processing is carried out by the operator of this application. Contact details can be found in the Legal Notice / Imprint.

3. What Data We Collect

Account data
The email address and password you provide during registration. Passwords are never stored in plain text — Firebase Authentication stores only a securely hashed version.

App content
Any contacts, tasks, and subtasks you create or edit while using the app, which you provide voluntarily as part of using the Product.

Technical/session data
Firebase Authentication stores a session token locally in your browser (via IndexedDB/local storage) to keep you signed in. This is functional, not used for tracking or advertising.

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising cookies, or any third-party tracking or profiling tools.

4. Purpose and Legal Basis

Your data is processed solely to provide the Product's core functionality (account creation, login, and task/contact management), on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (processing necessary for the performance of a contract/service you requested by creating an account).

5. Third-Party Processor: Google Firebase

This application uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Your account and app data are stored on Google's servers. Depending on Google's infrastructure, data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States, on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More information: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Data Processing Terms.

6. Storage Duration

Your account and associated data are retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Cookies

This application does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Firebase Authentication may use local browser storage (not classic cookies) strictly to keep you logged in between sessions.

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to: access your stored data free of charge; request correction or deletion of your data; restrict or object to processing; and data portability. To exercise these rights, or to request account deletion, contact us using the details in the Legal Notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Educational Project Notice

Join was developed as part of a student bootcamp project at Developer Akademie GmbH and is provided for educational and demonstration purposes. As such, we cannot guarantee continuous availability, data durability, or long-term data retention.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal requirements or changes to the Product's functionality.
